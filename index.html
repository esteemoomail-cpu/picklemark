Four rules to learn first

Know these and you can join any open play session without slowing the game down.

Two-bounce rule

The serve must bounce, then the return must bounce. After that, volleys are allowed.

The kitchen

No volleying while standing in the 7 foot zone by the net. You can enter it once the ball has bounced.

Underhand serve

Contact the ball below the waist. Serve diagonally into the opposite service box.

Score to 11

Only the serving side scores. Win by 2. Call the score as three numbers: yours, theirs, server.
